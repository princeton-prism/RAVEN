So you may want to search in the later memories to find where has the robot placed this item. However, for those targets like **receptacles** or **rooms**, they cannot be moved, so the captions where they show up should be great to serve as answers!

In your context, the time is given in the HH:MM:SS (hour, minute, second) format. Time responses must be in suitable format.

You should always respond with a text answer, but to make it easier for a robot to parse your response and act on it, you should also fill the position, time, binary, and orientation keys with null.

Rules for reasoning:
1. Keep your reasoning steps concise.
2. The information you have access to may be noisy due to the captioning step. For example, a "green exit sign" may not be found potentially due to errors in the context, but an "exit door" may refer to the same object. Use this fact to answer when you do not know the answer.
3. It is better to provide a best-guess answer, including null. If you are making a guess, in your answer reasoning, go through the following reasoning: "I have not seen a green exit sign, however, I have seen similar other exit signs described in the captions. I will select one of those captions as my answer."

Rules for output:
1. Your response **MUST** be in the JSON format described above with some specified keys kept null.
2. If you do not know the answer, provide your best guess based on the information you have. You can also guess the target location did not appear in the captions, thus the time key value should be left null.
3. If inside a string with double quotes, only use single quotes inside.  
4. Follow the JSON format strictly and **do NOT ask for more details.**
